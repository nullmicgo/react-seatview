# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Single `index.html` with Tailwind CSS and Three.js, no build step. Confirmed by the user so the preview can be opened from a local server immediately.

## Users

High-net-worth members of a private club or theatre, before they arrive. Their job is to choose one VIP room, see it from inside, and set the scent and drink they want waiting.

## Product Purpose

A member previews three distinct VIP rooms in one place, enters the chosen room through a camera move, reads how that room feels, and records arrival preferences. Success is a confident room choice plus preferences saved for the visit, not a completed payment.

## Positioning

The floor plan and the inhabited view are the same decision. Choosing a room on the plan is what moves the member inside it. A neighbouring seat map can show a diagram; this one delivers the view from the chosen place, with the room's own measures beside it.

## Operating Context

Used before arrival, on a desktop browser. Three rooms are always present: The Penthouse Suite, The Zen Chamber, and The Grand Lounge. The member compares them on a plan, inhabits one, then sets scent and drink. No booking desk, payment flow, or live club system is connected.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- An interactive plan of the three rooms.
- A smooth camera fly-in from the plan into a first-person view of the chosen room, and a way back to the plan.
- Live room measures shown as demonstration data: distance, privacy index, the active scent, and whether a private butler is assigned.
- A drawer for arrival preferences: scent and drink. The choice stays visible after it is confirmed.
- No prices, membership fees, real venue name, real staff, testimonials, or booking confirmation. Any numbers on screen are synthetic demonstration data and must stay labelled as such.
- Interface language was not confirmed separately. The request is in Cantonese and the three room names are English. Working assumption for this build: Traditional Chinese interface copy, with those English room names kept.

## Brand Commitments

The user made these binding: a dark theme, champagne-gold accents, frosted gold frames, small motion on interaction, and the tone of a private room reserved for a high-net-worth guest. The three room names above are fixed. The setting is a theatre or a distinguished VIP club, not a public box office.

## Evidence on Hand

No photography, floor-plan survey, menu, price list, or club identity was provided. Room geometry, measures, scents, and drinks are authored demonstration material. Do not present them as a real venue's records.

## Product Principles

- The member chooses a room by inhabiting it.
- Measures describe the room. They do not sell it.
- Preferences are set before arrival and remain visible after they are confirmed.
- Demonstration figures stay marked as demonstration.
- The three rooms stay different from each other in character, not only in name.
